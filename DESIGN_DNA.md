# DESIGN_DNA: Irwin G. Pasternack, AIA + Associates concept

**One line:** a 50 year Phoenix firm with 4,270 projects, drawn like its own construction set.

| Choice | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero archetype | Asymmetric: a giant counted "4,270+" on a drafting grid at left, a photo bleeding off the right edge with a sheet number tag | The scale of the record is the story. |
| Display / body | Big Shoulders Display 600/800 + IBM Plex Sans 400/600 | Condensed technical display, engineering sans. |
| Palette | `--blueprint` #142a40, `--sky` #3d7fb8, `--sand` #c8a27a, `--caliche` #f4efe6, `--terra` #b5532e, `--saguaro` #4f6b4a, `--muted` #59585a | Arizona sky and sandstone from the firm's photography, blueprint navy. |
| Signature | The dimension line and sheet numbers (A 101, A 201...), the drafting grid, and a title block footer | |
| Grid break | Home: hero photo bleeds off the page; the disciplines grid staggers up and down. Work: alternating sides. | |
| Footer | An architectural title block with the office details | |
